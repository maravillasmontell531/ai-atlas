# AI Atlas Taxonomy

Resource types: product (user-facing workflow); model_family (related model releases); model_checkpoint (exact artifact); api_provider (hosted access); framework (workflow toolkit); library (reusable component); dataset (collection); benchmark (test protocol); infrastructure (compute/serving/storage/registry); research_resource (research archive/database); protocol (interoperability); learning_resource (education); company (organization).

## Categories

- **Assistants and General-Purpose AI:** General chat, multimodal assistants, workplace copilots and local workspaces
- **Search, Research and Knowledge Discovery:** Web research, cited answers, scholarly search, literature review and computational knowledge
- **Coding and Software Engineering:** AI IDEs, terminal agents, code review, app generation, testing and benchmarks
- **Writing and Productivity:** Writing, meetings, email, documents, presentations, PDFs and knowledge management
- **Image Generation, Design and Vision:** Image models, generative editing, graphic design, UI, OCR and computer vision
- **Video, Avatars, Animation and 3D:** Video generation/editing, synthetic presenters, motion capture, animation and 3D assets
- **Speech, Voice, Audio and Music:** Speech recognition/synthesis, voice agents, music generation and sound restoration
- **Agents, Automation and Tool Protocols:** Agent frameworks, orchestration, browser agents, workflow automation, connectors and guardrails
- **AI APIs, Providers and Inference Platforms:** Model APIs, hosted inference, multi-model routers, GPU serving and enterprise cloud platforms
- **Open Models, Local AI and Model Development:** Model hubs, local runtimes, serving engines, fine-tuning, optimization, tracking and MLOps
- **Data, RAG, Evaluation and AI Security:** Document parsing, vector search, retrieval, embeddings, evaluation, observability and AI security
- **Education, Science and Industry AI:** Education, math tools, biology, healthcare, legal, finance, enterprise and climate applications

## Modalities

Use documented input/output tags such as text, code, image, audio, speech, video, 3D, spatial, tabular, graph or multimodal. Upload controls do not prove native processing for every format.

## Deployment and license

Possible deployment labels include hosted app, API, self-hosted, local desktop, on-device, cloud and hybrid. Distinguish open-source software, open weights, and open data; save the exact license and terms separately. Free trial is not a license.

## Verification states

seed = discovery lead; needs_review = ambiguous/incomplete; verified = checked against primary sources on a dated review; stale = facts may have changed; archived = retired/superseded. Status is metadata confidence, not product quality.
