# Sources, Verification, and Data Quality

AI Atlas aims to be broad without sacrificing reliability. A large directory is only useful when its links and claims are understandable and reasonably current.

## Source priority

Use sources in roughly this order:

1. **Official product pages** for product features and intended use.
2. **Official documentation and API references** for technical capabilities and integrations.
3. **Model cards, release notes, and official repositories** for model details, supported tasks, licenses, and limitations.
4. **Official pricing and terms pages** for costs, availability, commercial-use terms, and regional restrictions.
5. **Independent evaluations and reputable reporting** for comparisons or context not established by official sources.
6. **Community discussion** as a lead for investigation, not as the sole authority for consequential claims.

## Verify the right thing

- Check that each link points to the intended resource.
- Confirm whether a capability belongs to the model, application, or third-party integration.
- Verify prices against current official pages and note relevant plan or usage conditions.
- Read the license for the specific model or release, not just the provider's general statement.
- Record meaningful access limits, supported regions, context limits, file limits, and hardware requirements when relevant.
- Avoid copied marketing copy, private content, credentials, or unnecessary personal data.

## Suggested status values

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Verified | Key details were checked against a reliable source on the recorded date |
| Needs review | Some details may be stale or incomplete |
| Unavailable | The official destination appears unavailable; confirm before removing |
| Discontinued | A reliable source indicates the resource was discontinued |
| Community resource | Maintained by a community rather than an official vendor |

## Dates

Use ISO dates in YYYY-MM-DD format. Do not say information was checked today unless it was actually checked today. If a date is unknown, omit it or state Unknown.

## Claims and comparisons

- A capability is not a guarantee of accuracy.
- Benchmarks depend on methodology, model version, settings, and evaluation data.
- Pricing can vary by region, plan, currency, taxes, and usage.
- Open source, open weights, free, and commercially usable are not interchangeable.
- Avoid rankings unless the criteria, evidence, and date are clear.

## Images, logos, and trademarks

Prefer links to official brand assets or use text links when permission is unclear. Do not assume that a publicly accessible logo or image is free to redistribute. Trademarks may be used to identify the corresponding resources, but AI Atlas does not imply sponsorship or endorsement.

## Corrections

If you find a broken link, stale description, inaccurate capability, changed license, or discontinued product, open an issue or submit a pull request with the affected entry and a source supporting the correction.
