## How to compare resources

Test representative inputs, difficult edge cases, and failure recovery. Record exact model/version, plan, date, settings, latency, whole-workflow cost and scoring method. Check integration effort, privacy/retention, license and geographic access. Do not infer native file support from an upload control or commercial permission from public weights.
